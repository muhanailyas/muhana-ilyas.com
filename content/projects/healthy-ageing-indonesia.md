## What it is

A series of studies with geriatric medicine colleagues on how to measure ageing well in Indonesia, using tools that are cheap and quick enough for routine clinics.

## Why it matters

Indonesia's population is ageing quickly, but many assessment tools were developed and validated elsewhere. Before a tool can guide care, we need to know that it measures the same thing, reliably, in older Indonesians.

## Studies

- **Handgrip strength and ageing markers:** grip strength as an indicator of ageing, linked to ageing-related laboratory parameters. [*Frontiers in Medicine*, 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2025.1491584)
- **Dynamometer comparison:** agreement between digital handgrip dynamometers. [*BMJ Supportive & Palliative Care*, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1136/spcare-2024-005245)
- **Body composition:** anthropometry, body composition and grip strength. [*Journal of Clinical Medicine*, 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm13164697)
- **Sleep:** validity and reliability of the Insomnia Severity Index. [*PeerJ*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.20473)
- **Age-friendly cities:** validation of the Indonesian age-friendly cities and communities questionnaire. [*PLOS One*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0358251)
