Patients and clinicians often ask the same question before brain tumour surgery: what will this mean for memory, mood and everyday life? The published evidence exists, but it is scattered across small cohorts that report outcomes in different ways.

APONO pools these studies by tumour type and maps the reported outcomes onto the MNI152 template using standard parcellations, so that results can be read by location, time since treatment and treatment modality. The programme runs as a series of tumour-specific systematic reviews and meta-analyses, starting with intracranial meningioma, followed by glioma, pituitary adenoma, vestibular schwannoma, medulloblastoma and brain metastases, and ends with an open interactive web resource.
