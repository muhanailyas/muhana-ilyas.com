## What it is

Riset Inklusif is a research capacity-building platform I founded. It teaches clinical research methods to doctors and medical students in Indonesia, with a focus on evidence synthesis: systematic reviews, meta-analyses, scoping reviews and bibliometric reviews.

## Why it matters

Many clinicians in Indonesia have good research questions but little access to methods training, especially outside large academic centres. *Inklusif* means inclusive: the aim is that anyone with a clinical question can learn to answer it rigorously and publish the result.

## What we do

- **Courses** on study design, systematic review and meta-analysis, scoping and bibliometric reviews, AI and large language models in research, and publication ethics.
- **Mentoring** on real projects, from protocol to submission.
- **Open materials**, such as slides and handouts that teachers and students can reuse.
