APONO brings together published studies on adult brain tumours and maps postoperative cognitive, psychological and quality-of-life outcomes onto standard brain space (MNI152), so that clinicians, patients and researchers can see which outcomes have been reported for tumours in each region.

The first module covers intracranial meningioma. Glioma, pituitary adenoma, vestibular schwannoma, medulloblastoma and brain metastases will follow.
